How to Update Scores

Steps to update the leaderboard

1
Go to the ESPN Masters leaderboard at espn.com/golf/leaderboard and copy the leaderboard table. Start your highlight from the first player row (not the column header row) and drag down to the last player — on the final row, stop your highlight slightly before the right edge of the last column, as shown in the screenshot below.
Screenshot loading…
2
Open the Google Sheet using the link below (this is the same link shared on WhatsApp):
docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1JrZKNO6vkoqXBSY9aoDtviZz6WVnprODF1mGKmqUHc0/edit
3
Paste into cell A2. Click on cell A2 and paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V). Before doing anything else, scroll through the pasted data and find the projected cut row (it will appear as a divider row mid-table, not a player row) — select that entire row and delete it. Once deleted, rows 3 onwards will be shifted one column to the right. To fix this: select all cells from B3 downwards (to the last row of data), cut them (Ctrl+X / Cmd+X), then click cell A3 and paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V). This moves everything back into the correct columns.
4
The app updates automatically. The Google Sheet saves instantly, and the app refreshes every 3 minutes. If you want to see the update sooner, click the ↻ Refresh button on the Official Leaderboard tab — though it may still take up to 30 seconds for Google's servers to reflect the new data.
Note: Each time you update, paste from A2 and overwrite all previous data. You don't need to delete the old rows first — just paste over them.
