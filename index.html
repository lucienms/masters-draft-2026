How it works

1
Set up your Google Sheet with the columns below in row 1, matching exactly what ESPN's leaderboard shows. Paste ESPN data from row 2 onwards — you can select all & paste directly.
A: PosB: NameC: TotalD: TodayE: ThruF: R1G: R2H: R3I: R4J: Total Shots

2
Publish your sheet to web. In Google Sheets go to File → Share → Publish to web → select Sheet1 and CSV → click Publish. Copy the URL it gives you (it starts with https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX…).
3
Your published URL is already connected below. If you ever need to reconnect with a new URL, paste it here and click Connect. The app auto-refreshes every 3 minutes.
Updating scores: Whenever you want to refresh, open your Google Sheet, go to ESPN's Masters leaderboard, select all the leaderboard rows, copy and paste them into the sheet from row 2 — overwriting any previous data. Then click ↻ Refresh in the app (or wait up to 3 minutes for the auto-refresh). The app matches player names automatically.
